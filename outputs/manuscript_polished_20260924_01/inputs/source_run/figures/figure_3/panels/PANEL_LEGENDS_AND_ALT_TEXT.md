# a Shannon entropy and genome size

TE evenness tends to decrease with genome size across the 21 overlapping species. Shannon entropy (a) and Gini–Simpson index (b) summarize each species' ten most abundant positive classified dnaPipeTE superfamilies, reclosed to sum to one. Species points are coloured and shaped by reproductive strategy; a single full-sample Pagel-lambda PGLS is fitted per metric, without subgroup selection. Six reference species are labelled in the overview; all 21 are labelled in the isolated panels. Label selection changes no observations or fits. Lines and gray ribbons show the fitted mean and 95% coefficient confidence band. Annotations give unstandardized slopes per pg/1C, 95% t intervals, unadjusted P values, species n, and fitted lambda. Horizontal bars show released conditional measurement intervals. Genome-size estimates are IOD-derived and D. fuscus assembly-anchored, not independent species C-value measurements; anchor uncertainty is excluded. Predictor measurement error is displayed but not propagated into the fits. The supplied dated tree is preserved, with unresolved calibration/citation provenance. These companion diversity metrics are not independent replications and do not establish causal effects.

Alt text: All 21 species have labelled Shannon points and genome-size intervals around a negative full-sample PGLS line.
