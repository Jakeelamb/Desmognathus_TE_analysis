# a LTR fraction decomposition

Prespecified decomposition and phylogenetic analysis of the low-divergence LTR signature for all 34 TE species. (a) Each row shows total LTR hit bp / all RepeatMasker hit bp, below 5% LTR hit bp / all LTR hit bp, and below 5% LTR hit bp / all hit bp. Fractions satisfy the exact multiplicative identity; percentage values must be divided by 100 before multiplying. Axes differ among components. All available divergence bins enter the LTR denominator. The primary endpoint is the middle within-LTR fraction. It isolates profile shape from overall LTR representation in this pipeline, without estimating genomic occupancy or recent activity. The previous all-hit endpoint remains in the right panel and exported denominator sensitivities. (b) Primary endpoint against maximum SVL, with Pagel-lambda PGLS group curves from logit(fraction)~strategy+log10(SVL), limited to each group's observed SVL range; shading shows conditional 95% confidence intervals. (c) Raw-logit contrasts relative to aquatic eggs, with and without SVL. Groups are 7 aquatic-egg species, 24 terrestrial-egg species with aquatic larvae, and 3 direct developers. Intervals use t_(n-p) and condition on fitted lambda. The omnibus test compares strategy+SVL versus SVL by approximate ML likelihood ratio with 2 df; the unadjusted test is also exported. The three reproductive strategies were expert-coded after PCA inspection, and a group count is not a count of independent evolutionary transitions. No slope interaction, species filtering, divergence cutoff search or TE family screening was performed. All associations are exploratory; neither group separation nor low divergence establishes insertion timing or causation. Colors and species order match earlier TE figures; all 34 have maximum SVL. Trait uncertainty and uncertain source-tree calibration are not propagated. RepeatMasker aligned-hit denominators and dnaPipeTE quality denominators are kept distinct.

Alt text: Three aligned phylogenetic species tracks decompose the all-hit low-divergence LTR share into total LTR representation and within-LTR low-divergence fraction.
