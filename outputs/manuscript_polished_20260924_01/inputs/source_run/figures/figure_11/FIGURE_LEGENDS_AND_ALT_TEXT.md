# Repeat divergence and the genome–cell relationship

Focused exploratory integration of one prespecified repeat-divergence signature with genome size, cell area, nucleus area and maximum SVL. The primary TE score is RepeatMasker LTR hit bp below 5% divergence divided by all LTR hit bp, across all available divergence bins; it is not TE load, insertion rate or age. (a) All 21 complete species with genome-size measurement intervals, a marginal PGLS curve (dashed) and an SVL-adjusted curve (solid) at the geometric mean SVL of these 21 species. Gray shading is a conditional 95% confidence band. Adjusted curves need not follow the marginal point cloud. (b) TE coefficients from g~t+b, c~g+t+b, and n~c+g+t+b, where b is maximum SVL. The vertical bar denotes conditioning, not a causal direction. All use the same 21 species. (c) Genome-to-cell and the joint cell/genome-to-nucleus coefficients compare existing full24 body-adjusted fits, matched21 body-adjusted fits, and matched21 fits additionally including the TE score. Joint nuclear models retain both cell and genome predictors. Traits use the existing full24 log10 standardization; the logit TE fraction uses full34 standardization. Pagel-lambda ML regressions use t_(n-p) confidence intervals conditional on fitted lambda. Missing TE data for D. brimleyorum, D. folkertsi and D. ochrophaeus are not imputed. The all-hit denominator sensitivity and every equation, coefficient,lambda, warning and species inclusion are exported. Genome-size calibration, individual-animal uncertainty, SVL uncertainty and tree provenance remain as defined for prior figures. Associations do not establish an ordered TE-to-genome-to-cell causal chain.

Alt text: A 21-species genome-size scatterplot and two coefficient panels link the fixed LTR-divergence signature with existing cellular relationships.
