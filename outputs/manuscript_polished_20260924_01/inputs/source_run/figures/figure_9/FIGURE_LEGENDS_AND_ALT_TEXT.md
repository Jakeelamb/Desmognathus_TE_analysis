# Animal-aware robustness of the cellular relationships

Animal-aware sensitivity of the body-adjusted analysis. (a) Animal counts printed inside points, positioned by the largest animal's share of objects in each species and assay; fixed phylogenetic species order. (b) Percent changes when morphology uses the median of animal medians and IOD the equal-animal mean of image medians. (c) Critical conditional PGLS coefficients with 95% t intervals for released and alternate summaries, full24 and the existing 22-species sensitivity. (d) Candidate-weight distributions for 250 hierarchical measurement draws and 24 leave-one-species-out fits; labels count first ranks. SVL is upstream in all four candidates. The morphology panel contains selected reviewed large cells; paired areas remain paired during resampling. Assays are independently resampled; cross-assay covariance is unmodeled. Single-animal species provide no estimate of between-animal uncertainty. Measurement-sensitivity ranges are not complete uncertainty intervals. See analysis/README.md for estimands and anchoring.
