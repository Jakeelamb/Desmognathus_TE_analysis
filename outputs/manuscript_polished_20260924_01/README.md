The original galleries and their notes remain at ports 8766 and 8767. This version has its own notes and gallery at **http://127.0.0.1:8768/**.

GBE permits eight combined main-text figures and tables. This eight-figure version therefore places the tables in supporting material. If a main table is later selected, one figure must be combined or moved. All collages use Arial at 180 mm width and at most 245 mm height, with a working minimum of 7.5 pt. Exports include a vector PDF, a 300-dpi screen PNG, and a 600-dpi CMYK TIFF publication derivative. The TIFFs cover both color-art and black-and-white line-art resolution requirements. Each panel is also isolated. Biological color mappings and phylogenetic species order are shared. Three cellular species have known aquatic-larval development but no supplied egg-placement category and are explicitly unassigned for that distinction.
