# Shared repeat architecture across the radiation

A shared LTR-rich repeat composition spans 34 sampled Desmognathus species. (a) The supplied species tree is pruned and shown as topology only; branch lengths are omitted because citation and calibration provenance remain unresolved. Tip symbols identify reproductive strategy (7 aquatic-egg species, 24 terrestrial-egg species with aquatic larvae, and 3 direct developers). (b) Estimated genome size is available for 21 species; n/a marks the 13 unavailable estimates. Points and one-decimal labels show D. fuscus assembly-anchored, nuclear-IOD-derived pg/1C estimates; bars show conditional 95% measurement-bootstrap intervals. These are not independently measured species C-values; intervals exclude anchor uncertainty and are conditional on observed images and animals. Species-level support remains in the accompanying tables. (c) LTR, LINE, TIR and Other are shares of classified dnaPipeTE aligned bases after the released technical-run aggregation. Other combines the remaining classified orders; unresolved mass is outside this denominator. Fractions are not whole-genome occupancy. All panels use one fixed phylogenetic row order.

Alt text: Thirty-four species align across a topology-only tree with reproductive-strategy tip symbols, 21 estimated genome sizes with intervals and 13 explicit missing values, and stacked LTR, LINE, TIR and other classified repeat proportions.
