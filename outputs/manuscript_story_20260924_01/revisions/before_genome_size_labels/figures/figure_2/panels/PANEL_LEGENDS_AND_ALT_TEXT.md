# a Repeat-divergence profiles

Representation of low-divergence repeats differs across sampled reproductive strategies, with the clearest separation among LTRs. (a) LTR, LINE and TIR profiles show all 34 species as thin gray curves and equal-weight means for 7 aquatic-egg species, 24 terrestrial-egg species with aquatic larvae, and 3 direct developers as coloured curves. Each 1-percentage-point bin is expressed as its share of all RepeatMasker-aligned hit bp for that species; missing order/bin combinations are zero-filled across bins 0–49. All orders share 0–50% divergence and 0–3% aligned-hit-bp axes. No within-order normalization is applied. (b) For every species, the same all-hit denominator is used for the sum of bins 0–4, shown on matched 0–6% axes in the shared phylogenetic order. RepeatMasker alignments come from dnaPipeTE repeat-contig assemblies. Strategy comparisons are descriptive and not phylogenetically corrected. Sequence divergence is not insertion age or a measured transposition rate; aligned-hit fractions are not whole-genome occupancy. No biological confidence intervals are inferred from the aligned hits.

Alt text: Shared-axis LTR, LINE and TIR sequence-divergence profiles: gray species curves and coloured equal-weight reproductive-strategy means.

# b Low-divergence repeat representation

All 34 species retain the phylogenetic row order. Points sum bins 0–4 of the released RepeatMasker landscape for LTR, LINE and TIR and retain each species' full aligned-hit-bp denominator. The three axes are identically scaled 0–6%. Reproductive-strategy groupings are descriptive, with no new test or biological interval.

Alt text: All-species dot plot with three matched columns for LTR, LINE and TIR fractions below 5% divergence.
