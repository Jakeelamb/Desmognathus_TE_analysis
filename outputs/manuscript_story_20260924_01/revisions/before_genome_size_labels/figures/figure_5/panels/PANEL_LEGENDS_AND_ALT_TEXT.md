# a Cell area and estimated genome size

Estimated genome size is positively associated with selected erythrocyte area across the sampled species. All 24 species appear in each panel: cell area versus estimated genome size (a), nucleus area versus estimated genome size (b), and cell area versus nucleus area (c). The larger first panel labels all species; panels b and c label five reference species for orientation, while all species are labelled in the isolated exports. The exact released S26 Pagel-lambda PGLS coefficients and covariance matrices are reused without refitting. Fits use log10 traits followed by sample standardization, then are back-transformed for display. Annotations give standardized slopes, 95% t intervals and unadjusted P values; fitted lambda is approximately zero for all three fits. Black lines and gray ribbons show fitted means and 95% model confidence bands. Horizontal and vertical bars show released conditional measurement intervals. Cellular summaries describe selected large erythrocytes and their nuclei, based on 1,152 accepted pairs. Seven species have one-animal morphology support. Genome sizes are IOD-derived D. fuscus assembly-anchored estimates, not independent species C-values; three estimates have one-image/one-animal support and four fail the recorded IOD quality-balance gate (S24). Uniform circles retain every observation; support flags remain in the plotted-data export. Measurement intervals exclude anchor uncertainty and do not represent population-level sampling uncertainty. Associations do not establish causal direction.

Alt text: All 24 labelled species show cell-area and genome-size intervals, with a positive fitted relationship and confidence band.
