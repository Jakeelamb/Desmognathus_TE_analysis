# Marginal and conditional associations

Released S28 Pagel-lambda coefficients compare marginal and conditional associations, with standardized log10 traits and estimated genome size as predictor in all four regressions. The genome–nucleus coefficient is 0.41902 (95% normal interval 0.03960 to 0.79844) marginally, and 0.00792 (-0.49195 to 0.50780) after adjusting for cell area. The genome–cell coefficient is 0.71666 (0.42523 to 1.00810) marginally, and 0.57486 (0.27994 to 0.86977) after adjusting for nucleus area. Intervals are the stored S28 normal approximations (coefficient +/- 1.96 standard errors), not the t intervals used for the Figure 5 pairwise displays. The adjusted genome–nucleus P is 0.97551; the adjusted genome–cell P is 0.0009971. These estimates explain the contrasting independence tests but are not estimates of indirect causal effects. The broad adjusted genome–nucleus interval does not establish an exactly zero effect. No models were refitted; these conditional comparisons are distinct from the single-parent paths shown in the equivalent DAG orientations.
