# Simulation calibration of path-class recovery

All eight released calibration settings are shown: four generating classes under Brownian and independent residual regimes, with 100 simulations each. True-class top-recovery rates are cell bridge 78%/80%, nucleus bridge 49%/41%, genome bridge 29%/33%, and independence 40%/36% (Brownian/independent). These are recovery frequencies under the specified simulation settings, not posterior probabilities for the observed causal directions.
