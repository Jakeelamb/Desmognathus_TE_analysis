# Alternative positions of body size in the path analysis

(a) Eight biologically motivated candidates are compared on the same four traits using Pagel-lambda phylogenetic path analysis. All log10 traits retain the full24 means and sample SDs. Full24 and the targeted exclusion of aeneus and wrighti (n=22) are fitted separately. Primary test ordering is b,g,c,n; a prespecified reverse n,c,g,b checks sensitivity to regression direction with estimated lambda for node pairs without a directed path. Ancestral pairs retain the graph-implied test direction under phylopath. Delta CICc and normalized weights apply only within one cohort, order and eight-candidate set. The earlier four-candidate weights remain valid within that earlier set. No saturated graph or exhaustive search is ranked. Higher global-fit P indicates no detected contradiction of the tested claims, not proof of a causal model. (b) Candidate arrows define representatives of eight distinct Markov equivalence classes. Equivalence is checked by the exact undirected skeleton and unshielded colliders; all orientations of each small skeleton are enumerated and cyclic graphs rejected. Counts describe equivalent acyclic orientations, not additional fitted hypotheses. Body-size downstream models and alternative cellular chains broaden the earlier upstream-only assumption. (c) The full24 primary winner is cell_body_branch, weight 0.524030 and global-fit P 0.423708. Its class has 4 acyclic orientations with identical observational conditional independences. The representative and up to two reorientations are drawn to make this causal nonidentifiability visible. These schematic arrows have no effect-size encoding. Equivalence is a graphical statement; separately fitted reversed phylogenetic regressions may differ numerically because their residual covariance parameters are estimated.

Alt text: Alternative body-size roles in a finite eight-class phylogenetic path comparison, with an atlas of hypotheses and explicit equivalent orientations.
