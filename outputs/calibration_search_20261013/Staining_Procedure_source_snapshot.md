Staining and Imaging Procedure

**Staining**  
1\. Fixation	24 hr	85 methanol:10 formalin:5 acetic acid (MFA).  
2\. Rinse	10 min	Tepid running tapwater.  
3\. Hydrolysis	120 min	5.0 N HCl.  
4\. Short rinse	Dip	0.1 N HCl.  
5\. Stain	120 min	Schiff reagent.  
6\. Bisulfite rinse	5 min × 3	3 changes of fresh bisulfite solution.  
7\. Rinse	10 min	Tepid running tapwater.  
8\. Rinse	2 min × 3	3 changes of distilled water.  
9\. Dry	N/A	Air-dry and store in the dark.

Recipes for the preparation of Schiff reagent and bisulfite water to Feulgen stain 100 slides per run in 1 L staining boats. Note that three runs can easily be done simultaneously, for a total of 300 slides per batch. Stains made with different dye lots should be tested before use by staining standards of known DNA content ratios. Bisulfite water must be prepared fresh for each staining run.

**Imaging**

1) Make sure the microscope is clean  
2) Set light wavelength to monochromatic \~560nm  
3) Set exposure time to 1/125  
4) White balance on a blank area of the slide  
5) Find a field containing nuclei  
6) Green channel only should be used for the IOD measurements
