Review rubric

  1. Inspect the whole nucleus with the mask hidden.
  2. Compare the mask with the visible boundary, including faint edges.
  3. Check for clipping, neighboring nuclei, overlap, and included debris.

Keep: a complete, isolated nucleus with a supported mask. Problem: a visible defect. Unsure: the pixels do not support a confident judgment. Review does not validate a measurement or calibration.
