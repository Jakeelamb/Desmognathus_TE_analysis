# Wang 2025 Figure 3 versus the current LTR figures

Verified 2026-09-24. This is a source comparison, not a new analysis. No figure, numerical release, or manuscript Methods file was changed.

## Paper evidence

Wang et al. use LTRpred plus manual BLAST checks on LTR/Gypsy contigs >3,000 bp; internal regions span first-to-last TE domains. Five species contribute 12–25 contigs each. Bowtie2 very-sensitive-local comparisons vary G (20/30/40/50) and unpaired versus merged-plus-unmerged reads. Ratios use length-normalized mapping hits. Figure 3 has vertical log-scale violins ordered by genome size, medians 0.43–0.94, and species ANOVA F(4,85) = 2.11, P = 0.087. Its 1:1 line denotes the no-deletion expectation. [Paper: Figure 3, Results, and deletion Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08127-3#Fig3).

## Verified differences in this project

- **Sampling and selection.** Our primary panel contains 380 elements from 30 Desmognathus species, with 3–50 elements per species. Selection requires length ≥3,000 bp, an exact sequence-and-coordinate TEsorter join, LTR/Gypsy classification, and ≥5 correctly parsed domain annotations. The relaxed comparison retains 989 elements (11–113 per species) using ≥4 domains. Both branches apply inclusive within-species 1.5-IQR fences before intersecting them with ≥80% positive-depth coverage in each LTR and the internal region. This sensitivity addresses domain eligibility, not mapping-stringency sensitivity. [Selection and counts](../../analyses/01_transposable_elements/ltr_filter_comparison.R), [released counts](../../Publication/figures/candidates/figure_4_filter_comparison/analysis/branch_counts.csv).
- **The denominator differs materially.** Our archived calculator uses the **entire interval between the two annotated LTRs**, including any sequence outside the protein-domain span. It averages per-base depth over that interval. Terminal depth is the length-weighted average across both LTRs; explicit zeros and unreported positions are included. Thus our per-base-depth implementation cannot be assumed numerically identical to the paper's normalized-hit statistic. [Archived calculator, `calculate_zero_aware_depth_metrics`, lines 107–150](../../../Desmognathus_TE_archive/scripts/processing/build_corrected_ectopic_recombination.py).
- **Upstream provenance is incomplete.** The draft describes DANTE/LTRharvest/LTRdigest, but the active replay starts from frozen element/depth summaries; this audit does not verify the original discovery execution or manual structural checks. Recovered mapping information supports paired, trimmed, mitochondrial-filtered reads and Bowtie2 `-L 20 --very-sensitive-local`, followed by SAMtools depth processing. Exact versions, MAPQ, multimapper, secondary/supplementary, and duplicate policies remain unresolved. Our recorded `-L 20` is seed length and does not establish the paper's G settings. We have not reproduced its mapping or read-treatment comparisons. [Release provenance](../../analyses/01_transposable_elements/provenance/ltr_terminal_internal_release.json), [explicit upstream boundary](../../analyses/01_transposable_elements/README.md#frozen-and-archive-only-upstream-work).
- **Figures and inferential unit.** Our horizontal violins follow the shared phylogenetic order and show raw elements, medians, element-bootstrap intervals, and counts. The genome-size companion uses one median per species for the 19 species with estimated genome sizes and fits log–log PGLS with fitted Pagel's lambda. The primary slope is 0.024 (95% CI −0.561 to 0.609; P = 0.931); the relaxed branch gives P = 0.925. Species-level measurement intervals are displayed but not propagated into the fitted model. [Figure contract](../../scripts/publication/figure4_panels.R), [PGLS implementation](../../analyses/01_transposable_elements/ltr_genome_pgls.R), [comparison results](../../Publication/figures/candidates/figure_4_filter_comparison/analysis/pgls_model_summary.csv).

## Interpretation

Our primary medians span 0.433–0.900, so values below one are not by themselves a contradiction of the motivating paper. Nevertheless, neither the distribution nor a nonsignificant P-value validates an absolute deletion-rate calibration. For this project, 1:1 is equal mapped terminal and internal mean depth; the statistic remains a mapping/deletion-footprint proxy. The PGLS asks whether that species-level proxy changes with genome size while accounting for shared ancestry. An ordinary species ANOVA asks whether group means differ and does not replace that continuous phylogenetic association test. Neither nonsignificant result proves biological equivalence. [Species summaries](../../Publication/figures/candidates/figure_4_filter_comparison/analysis/species_summaries.csv), [declared interpretation boundary](../../analyses/01_transposable_elements/README.md#interpretation-boundary).

The most informative future comparability check would use matched terminal/internal boundaries and documented mapping settings, then evaluate mapping and filtering sensitivity separately. This is a recommendation, not an analysis performed here.
