# Feulgen recovery pilot: software shortlist and validation contract

Research checked **2026-10-13**, against paper HEAD `8a5c00b33c0359b4a47f2df2ead145039554d623`. This is a tooling recommendation, not a tested installation or calibration decision. No software was installed, no producer was executed, and no retained pixels, masks, Methods, measurements or figures were edited.

That statement describes the research phase. The subsequent implementation
uses pinned ImageJ 1.54p for ROI rasterization and manual editing, a Rust reducer
for numerical measurements, and a separate Pixi `recovery` environment. It does
not install the full Fiji distribution or Bio-Formats. See the
[recovery workflow](../MICROSCOPY_RECOVERY.md) for the implemented scope and
verification evidence.

## Recommendation and existing evidence

Start with **Fiji/Bio-Formats for source inspection and manual contour annotation; the existing locked Pixi `image-qc` environment for interchange and original-pixel measurement; the retained blinded reviewer for decisions**. Add QuPath or napari only if a bounded editor trial shows a concrete benefit. Defer learned segmentation and retrospective shading correction until manual repairs establish a reference set.

Observed repository facts: [STANDARD_QC.md](../STANDARD_QC.md) specifies a census of **47** standard objects, a 40-object frozen quality template, blank default decisions and no automatic acceptance. The retained [pixel audit execution record](../../outputs/standard_calibration_review_20261013/PIXEL_AUDIT_EXECUTION.md) reports 31 Process 413 and 16 Process 414 objects, complete recorded-label reconstruction and partial biological-boundary uncertainty; its historical producer HEAD was `15dfb3c18755cc8659b626fa213087c62c6db02f`, not this research HEAD. Its numerical parity evidence does not establish complete nuclei. `scripts/publication_metadata.py` records the **805 reviewed target nuclei**. Keep those frozen; do not describe a 27-object view as the complete 47-object census.

[pixi.toml](../../pixi.toml), `feature.image-qc.pypi-dependencies`, already pins Pillow 12.3.0, tifffile 2026.9.20, roifile 2026.9.22, scikit-image 0.26.0, SciPy 1.17.1 and matplotlib 3.11.0. These are declared/locked dependencies, not proof that every package or GUI works on this machine. Repository Pixi policy supersedes upstream pip/Conda instructions.

## Source pixels and metadata

Bio-Formats standardizes acquisition metadata to OME; its CLI supports channel selection, uncompressed TIFF by default, compression selection and `-nooverwrite`. Those features make it a candidate extraction tool, not proof of losslessness for this archive. Record original file-group membership, source hashes, reader/version, series/resolution, channel index/order, dtype, dimensions, exposure and objective metadata when available. Keep original vendor metadata alongside OME-XML; absence of metadata must remain explicit. [Fiji features](https://bio-formats.readthedocs.io/en/stable/users/imagej/features.html), [conversion options](https://bio-formats.readthedocs.io/en/stable/users/comlinetools/conversion.html).

Especially relevant: Bio-Formats' **cellSens VSI** page rates pixel support “Fair”, lists the `CellSensReader`, and says the reader is not BSD licensed. Availability of a reader does not establish recoverability when the full original VSI/ETS file group is missing. Compare actual decoded green arrays against retained tiles at exact known offsets before trusting another import. [Format evidence](https://bio-formats.readthedocs.io/en/stable/formats/cellsens-vsi.html).

Pilot contract proposed here: quantitative pixels remain the original uint8 green plane. Display LUT/contrast can assist a reviewer, but saves must preserve raw values. No histogram equalization, denoising, sharpening, interpolation or model restoration becomes quantitative IOD input. ImageJ `RawIntDen` is a sum of pixel values; it is **not** the native optical-density IOD calculation. Use ImageJ to verify area/raw sums independently, then check the repository's pinned native IOD function, background and clipping policy separately. [ImageJ measurement definitions](https://imagej.net/ij/docs/menus/analyze.html).

## Contour and background interchange contract (proposed)

Use the full available raw tile/field and wider context to decide whether a complete biological nucleus is visible. Existing mask extent is only a starting proposal. Never extend a boundary into unavailable pixels; retain unresolved edge objects explicitly.

Retain editable ROI ZIP and a canonical full-resolution integer label TIFF, plus a JSON/CSV sidecar. ImageJ ROI Manager can open ROI ZIP and save all selections as ZIP; napari labels are integer-valued with background 0. GeoJSON is QuPath's preferred annotation export, but vector shape equivalence does not imply identical raster pixels. [ROI Manager](https://imagej.net/ij/docs/guide/146-30.html), [napari labels](https://napari.org/stable/howtos/layers/labels.html), [QuPath export](https://github.com/qupath/qupath-docs/blob/main/docs/advanced/exporting_annotations.md).

Sidecar fields: original composite identity; source hash and original dimensions; tile origin; explicit local/global coordinates; pixel units and axis order (`x,y` vectors versus `row,column` arrays); mask ID; contour version; author/reviewer/date; proposal parent; nuclear/cell/background class; decision and exclusion reason. Preserve subpixel vertices, holes and multipart geometry or reject unsupported input. Store the canonical raster hash and rasterization rule. Keep field-background ROIs as separate objects, with stain/cell/debris exclusions and their own IDs; do not infer a historical `nuc_i_bg` from a newly drawn background ROI.

Compare geometry-only repairs at the historical background first. Report background-policy sensitivity separately on unchanged raw pixels. Freeze background selection rules before seeing between-standard IOD agreement; selecting masks/backgrounds to force agreement would bias the pilot.

## Blinded review and QC (proposed)

Two reviewers independently annotate a shared, randomized, pseudonymous set, with standard/specimen identity, previous IOD and expected calibration hidden. Keep independent exports and a third adjudication version; do not overwrite either reviewer. QuPath can mask image names and hide thumbnails, but that UI feature alone cannot conceal source paths, metadata or exported project files. Fiji/napari need pseudonymous working copies and a separate coordinator-held mapping. Retain blank/unsure/problem decisions; tool-generated contours never imply acceptance. [QuPath project masking](https://qupath.readthedocs.io/en/latest/docs/tutorials/projects.html). Existing review semantics are documented in [STANDARD_QC.md](../STANDARD_QC.md).

QC should include full biological-boundary visibility, tile/field edge contact, adjacent-object separation, saturation counts at 0/255, background dispersion and spatial gradient, focus diagnostics, area, topology, changed-pixel counts and IOD/background sensitivity. No universal acceptance threshold is established here. Compare reviewer masks using Dice/Jaccard, area change, boundary-distance/Hausdorff and split/merge counts; compare derived IOD differences too. scikit-image supplies Hausdorff and segmentation-comparison metrics, but a high overlap score can still miss a small dark region important to IOD. [Metric API](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.metrics.html).

## Prospective camera/illumination work

EMVA 1288 **Release 4.0** distinguishes Linear and General modules and replaced 3.1 effective 2021-06-21. It supplies a primary camera-characterization framework, not validation of this microscope, stain chemistry or historical acquisition. [Official standard downloads](https://www.emva.org/standards-technology/emva-1288/emva-standard-1288-downloads-2/).

Proposed prospective evidence: identify the actual camera and acquisition settings; record whether gamma, automatic exposure, gain, white balance or compression is active; acquire repeat dark/blank fields and controlled exposure/illumination series; test green-channel response, saturation, repeatability and spatial nonuniformity under the actual microscope setup. Freeze exposures/settings and preserve unprocessed captures. This cannot retrospectively prove historical linearity.

BaSiC's official example notes correlated frames can require manual parameter changes; the method estimates correction from an image collection. Inference for this pilot: repetitive nuclei/limited fields can confound an estimated illumination map with specimen structure. A derived flatfield cannot recreate clipped intensities or prove the original camera response. Require held-out blank-field/known-response validation before quantitative correction; retain both raw and corrected sensitivity results. [BaSiCPy examples/backend](https://github.com/peng-lab/BaSiCPy). The original Nature paper was encountered but subsequent retrieval failed with its authentication redirect; no detailed paper claim is relied upon here.

## Meaningful behavior tests before admitting an editor or converter

These are proposed acceptance tests, **not executed** during this research.

1. **Decode/channel control:** independent readers decode a pinned source fixture; assert exact original uint8 green arrays, dimensions/axes and field offsets. Include asymmetric RGB sentinels to reject channel swaps, BGR assumptions, scaling and wrong series/resolution. Re-export/reopen losslessly and compare arrays, not just file hashes.
2. **Real editor round-trip:** draw/modify/reopen contours on a scratch raw tile. Compare canonical pixels for integer/subpixel, concave, holed and edge-touching fixtures. Reject an off-by-one origin, transposed axes, wrong image hash, duplicate identity, overlapping incompatible labels and silent instance-ID reassignment. Verify raw-source hashes unchanged.
3. **Original-pixel measurement:** known synthetic intensities/backgrounds give analytical area/raw-sum/OD results, including zero/saturated pixels and the native nonpositive-OD policy. Reproduce retained original-label IOD first; change one positive-OD mask pixel and background separately, proving expected sensitivity and rejection controls. Independent validator must implement the calculation independently rather than call the producer helper again.
4. **Blinding/decision control:** inspect UI and export for hidden labels; export/reopen blank, unsure and rejected cases. Confirm no automatic keep, no loss of the complete 47-object queue and no edits to the frozen 805-target table. Verify separate reviewer exports and adjudication linkage.
5. **Repeatability/provenance:** retain exact Pixi lock/task, editor/plugin version, source/ROI/mask/weight hashes, command/exit/time receipts and preservation hashes. Run only in a complete scratch checkout; output destinations must be new. A numerical/format PASS remains separate from human acceptance and absolute calibration.

## Unresolved availability and scope

No live GUI, source-format import, ROI round-trip, segmentation speed/accuracy, camera control or fresh acquisition was tested. Local installations and GPU support were not inventoried. Full original VSI/ETS recovery, reviewer availability, an independent specimen-specific DNA standard, historical camera response and old background justification remain external gaps. Broader model/tool acquisition has no demonstrated benefit for the current 47-object pilot.
