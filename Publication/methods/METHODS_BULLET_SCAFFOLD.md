## 3. TE composition, diversity, repeat landscapes, and LTR proxy

- `[VERIFIED]` Canonical diversity was calculated at the superfamily level. For each species, the ten most abundant strictly positive superfamilies were selected independently; descending-abundance ties were resolved deterministically by feature name, and the ten retained proportions were reclosed to one.
- `[VERIFIED]` S08 is one 34-row, four-column table in TE34 species order: `species`, `n_superfamilies_included`, `shannon_entropy`, and `gini_simpson`. `n_superfamilies_included` is ten for every species. Natural-log Shannon entropy was `H′ = −Σpᵢ ln(pᵢ)`, and the explicitly named Gini-Simpson index was `1 − Σpᵢ²`. These are the only paper-facing diversity indices.
- `[BOUNDARY]` Wang et al. (2025) state that diversity was calculated from the ten most abundant TE superfamilies but do not document whether those ten were reclosed. This study explicitly recloses its selected ten. Published and current values remain methodologically non-harmonized because their genomic inputs and annotation pipelines differ.
- `[BOUNDARY]` Diversity uses ten species-specific superfamilies, whereas the Bonsai representation and retained PCA audit use the 24 superfamilies positive in all 34 species. Unresolved aligned-base mass remains only in S06-S07 dnaPipeTE quality-control tables and enters neither calculation.
- `[VERIFIED]` TE34 superfamily PCA retained features with positive abundance in all 34 species after same-species retry averaging. Rows were closed and CLR-transformed, then columns were centered. The fit used no post-CLR variance scaling. Each axis was oriented so its largest absolute loading was positive. The retained matrix contains no zeros, so no zero replacement is required.
- `[VERIFIED]` S09 scores, S10 variance, and S11 loadings come from the retry-averaged 24-feature CLR-PCA fit and remain a supplementary geometry audit separate from the final eight main figures.
- `[VERIFIED]` The older seven-figure controlled release uses the same 34-species × 24-superfamily CLR matrix in the Bonsai implementation of de Groot et al. (2026), pinned to upstream commit `b16ef6f0a164610327022c7d0b92de82b3596aad`. The full fit disabled feature-variance rescaling, used exact all-pairs candidate search, 1,000 random NNI moves, and 10 random trees; no per-species feature-error matrix was supplied.
- `[VERIFIED]` S42 binds all 34 Bonsai tips to reproductive-strategy annotations, including the author-corrected *D. fuscus* assignment to terrestrial eggs with aquatic larvae. S43 records the fixed-tree and feature-resampling validation: 2 observed Fitch steps versus a 9.4132-step permutation-null mean across 99,999 label permutations (`P = 0.00001`; Holm-adjusted `P = 0.00009`).
- `[VERIFIED]` In 200 bounded feature-resampling refits, exact aquatic-egg and direct-development edges recurred in 55/200 (27.5%) and 70/200 (35.0%) fits, respectively; the terrestrial-egg + aquatic-larva strategy was exact in 2/200 (1.0%). Bounded refits used 100 random NNI moves and one random tree, versus 1,000 moves and 10 trees in the full fit.
- `[BOUNDARY]` The Bonsai Newick is a rooted display serialization of TE-composition similarity, not a species phylogeny. Branch lengths are fitted diffusion times, and the display root has no phylogenetic meaning.
- `[BOUNDARY]` Reproductive-strategy labels were supplied after review of the initial PCA. Fixed-tree label permutations test post-hoc localization, not causation or phylogenetically independent association. Resampling correlated CLR coordinates is a feature perturbation, not a biological-specimen bootstrap, and combines feature and bounded-search sensitivity.
- `[VERIFIED]` Formal clustering audit used all 23 nonzero, unscaled PCA axes, which preserve the complete 24-feature CLR/Aitchison distances; PC1–PC2 and PC1–PC6 fits were sensitivity analyses and were not used to select clusters.
- `[VERIFIED]` K-means diagnostics covered `k = 1…8` with 1,000 starts. The primary gap statistic used 1,000 scaled-PCA null datasets, squared Euclidean dispersion, the Tibshirani one-standard-error selector, and seed 20260810.
- `[VERIFIED]` No discrete cluster solution passed the rebuild gates. Gap selected `k=1`; maximum silhouette selected a weak `k=7` solution with two singleton clusters; the only singleton-free candidate was `k=2` (9/25 species, mean silhouette 0.179) and was unstable under 28-of-34 species subsampling (median adjusted Rand index -0.042).
- `[BOUNDARY]` The clustering gates were adopted during this post-hoc rebuild, not preregistered. Candidate `k=2` and `k=7` assignments are retained as rejected audit outputs and must not be described as supported biological clusters.
- `[VERIFIED]` Full 24-feature composition showed multivariate phylogenetic signal under Adams' `K_mult` with 99,999 complete-vector tip permutations: `K_mult = 0.6581`, `p = 0.00001`. Because `K_mult < 1`, signal was weaker than Brownian-motion expectation even though it exceeded the randomized-tip null.
