# Figure legends and alt text

## Figure_1

Twenty-one species are aligned in one fixed phylogenetic order across reproductive strategy, conditional genome size, dnaPipeTE composition, and RepeatMasker low-divergence signal. Panel a is a topology-only prune of the supplied species tree; branch lengths are omitted because the tree's publication, calibration, and time provenance remain unresolved. Panel b shows D. fuscus assembly-anchored IOD-derived pg/1C estimates and conditional intervals; crosses flag the prespecified image-quality balance caution and triangles flag one-image, one-animal support. These are not independently measured species C-values, and intervals exclude anchor uncertainty. Panel c shows broad-order shares of classified dnaPipeTE aligned bases. Panel d shows the same assay at the nine most abundant superfamilies, with bar height on one common scale. Panel e shows the RepeatMasker-aligned fraction below 5% sequence divergence for LTR, LINE, TIR, and all other annotations. dnaPipeTE composition and RepeatMasker landscape denominators are distinct; neither is genome occupancy, and divergence is not insertion age.

**Alt text:** A 21-tip topology-only species tree and reproductive-strategy rail align with conditional genome-size intervals, broad dnaPipeTE order composition, nine dnaPipeTE superfamily tracks, and a four-order heatmap of low-divergence RepeatMasker signal. Every row follows the same supplied-tree order, and symbols expose genome-size support cautions.

## Figure_2

Panel a shows the 34-tip Bonsai topology fitted to the retry-averaged 24-superfamily CLR matrix. Branch lengths are deliberately omitted from this main display so the full topology remains legible; the display root has no phylogenetic meaning, and this is not a species phylogeny. Tip symbols show the post-hoc reproductive-strategy annotation, including D. fuscus as terrestrial eggs with aquatic larvae. Only the exact aquatic-egg and direct-development edges are labeled with feature-resampling split support. Panel b shows exact-edge recovery with 95% binomial intervals across 200 bounded feature-resampling refits: aquatic eggs 55/200, direct development 70/200, and terrestrial eggs with aquatic larvae 2/200. Panel c shows the global strategy-label localization (2 observed Fitch steps versus a 9.41-step permutation-null mean; 99,999 permutations, P = 0.00001) and the post-hoc Aitchison-distance association (PERMANOVA R² = 0.260, P = 0.00001; PERMDISP P = 0.707). Ordinary label permutations are not phylogenetically corrected, and feature resampling perturbs compositional coordinates rather than biological specimens; the result is strategy-associated compositional structure, not evidence for discrete natural clusters or causation. Exact fitted branch lengths and PCA outputs remain supplementary audits.

**Alt text:** Panel a is a topology-only TE-composition tree for 34 species with all tips colored and shaped by post-hoc reproductive strategy; aquatic-egg species and the three direct developers localize to exact edges. Panel b gives exact-edge recovery estimates and binomial intervals from 200 feature refits. Panel c contrasts two observed Fitch steps with a permutation-null mean of 9.41 and summarizes the post-hoc multivariate association and dispersion diagnostic.

## Figure_3

RepeatMasker divergence profiles reveal both a shared low-divergence pulse and substantial among-species heterogeneity. Panel a shows LTR, LINE, and TIR profiles for all 34 species and the three post-hoc reproductive-strategy strata. Thin gray curves are individual species and colored curves are equal-weight group means after zero-filling absent category/bin combinations; facet y scales are independent, so magnitudes must be read from each numeric axis. The strata are descriptive and not a phylogenetically corrected test. Panel b shows each species' aligned-repeat fraction below 5% divergence for the same three orders, with species held in the shared supplied-tree order and strategy shown redundantly by color and shape. RepeatMasker divergence is not an insertion age, and aligned-repeat fractions are not genome occupancy.

**Alt text:** Panel a is a four-by-three matrix of individual-species and mean LTR, LINE, and TIR divergence profiles for all species and three reproductive strategies. Panel b is a 34-species heatmap of the aligned fraction below five percent divergence for those orders, with species in phylogenetic order and a reproductive-strategy rail.

## Figure_4

Panel a shows a topology-only 30-tip prune of the supplied 46-tip species tree; branch lengths are omitted because its publication or archive identifier, calibration method, and branch-length provenance remain unresolved. Species rows are derived from the plotted tip-y order and therefore follow the shared canonical phylogenetic order. Panel b shows the zero-aware terminal:internal LTR depth proxy across the exact 380-element primary branch. Hollow points are element-level observations, blue points are species medians, and horizontal blue intervals are 2,000-replicate element-bootstrap intervals. Elements are not biological replicates, and intervals are conditional on the recovered elements and sequencing resources. Elements were retained inclusively within each species' two-sided 1.5-IQR fences and only when the left LTR, right LTR, and internal region each had at least 80% positive-depth coverage. The primary branch derives from 408 LTR/Gypsy candidates at least 3,000 bp long with at least five correctly parsed TEsorter domain annotations. The dotted line marks equal terminal and internal mean depth (1:1); values to its right point in the direction expected under internal-sequence depletion. The tree supplies context and ordering only; this mapping/deletion-footprint proxy is not a phylogenetic analysis or an estimate of an ectopic-recombination, solo-LTR, or DNA-loss rate.

**Alt text:** Panel a shows a topology-only species tree pruned to the 30 LTR species. Panel b aligns the same 30 phylogenetic rows with 380 hollow element observations, species medians, and horizontal bootstrap intervals on a log2 terminal:internal ratio axis. A dotted vertical line marks equal terminal and internal depth, and light blue shading marks the direction expected under internal-sequence depletion.

## Figure_5

**Alt text:** A topology-only tree for 24 Desmognathus species aligns development mode with conditional genome-size, nucleus-area, and cell-area forest plots. All tracks share one phylogenetic row order, and point shapes expose single-animal and image-quality support cautions.

## Figure_6

Conditional genome size, nucleus area, and cell area are positively associated in Pagel-lambda PGLS sensitivity fits. Circles identify balanced multiple-image support, triangles identify estimates from one image and animal, and crosses identify four species that fail the declared quality-balance gate. Bars are conditional measurement-bootstrap intervals; lines and ribbons are PGLS fits and model confidence intervals. Genome size is the released D. fuscus assembly-anchored pg/1C estimate, not a direct independently measured species C-value.

**Alt text:** Three scatterplots show conditional genome size versus nucleus area, conditional genome size versus cell area, and nucleus area versus cell area, each with measurement intervals and a phylogenetically corrected fitted line; point shape exposes single-image and quality-balance limitations in the genome-size panels.
