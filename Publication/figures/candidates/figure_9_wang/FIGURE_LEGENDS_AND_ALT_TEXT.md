# Figure legends and alt text

## Figure_9

Rows show the canonical top-ten-superfamily Shannon entropy and Gini-Simpson index. Columns show all 21 overlap species, six aquatic-egg species, 13 terrestrial-egg species with aquatic larvae, and two direct developers. Hollow points are species and horizontal bars are estimated genome-size intervals. Lines and bands are Pagel-lambda PGLS fits and coefficient confidence bands; the n = 2 direct-development panels intentionally have no fit. Review candidate, not a numbered manuscript figure. All genome-size values are D. fuscus assembly-anchored IOD-derived estimates, not direct species C-values. Reproductive-strategy panels are post-hoc descriptive strata. The two direct developers are shown without a regression because n = 2.

**Alt text:** Two aligned rows of four framed scatterplots show Shannon and Gini-Simpson diversity against estimated genome size for all Desmognathus and three reproductive strategies. PGLS statistics appear inside six panels; the two direct-development panels show only their two observations and state that a fit was not estimated.
