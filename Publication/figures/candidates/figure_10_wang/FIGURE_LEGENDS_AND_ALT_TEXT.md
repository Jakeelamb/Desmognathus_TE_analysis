# Figure legends and alt text

## Figure_10

The supplied 24-tip Path24 tree (a) is shown without branch lengths, aligned to estimated D. fuscus-anchored genome-size (b), reviewed nucleus-area (c), and reviewed cell-area (d) estimates. Numbers label species estimates to one decimal; horizontal bars retain all released conditional measurement intervals. Axes are tightened to 8-27 pg/1C, 35-55 square micrometres, and 190-335 square micrometres respectively. The two sampled direct developers are D. aeneus and D. wrighti; the other 22 Path24 species have aquatic larvae and undergo metamorphosis. This is a descriptive visualization, not a development-mode test. Descriptive review only. D. organi is absent from Path24 and is not imputed. The genome-size track uses D. fuscus assembly-anchored pg/1C estimates, not direct independently measured species C-values. The tree shows topology only; publication and calibration provenance remain unresolved.

**Alt text:** A topology-only phylogeny for 24 Desmognathus species is aligned with labelled forest-plot tracks for estimated genome size, nucleus area, and cell area. D. aeneus and D. wrighti are the two direct developers represented in Path24.
