# Figure 6 isolated panels

Panels preserve the released data, PGLS fits, measurement intervals and scales.

## a - Genome size and nucleus area

Estimated genome size versus nucleus area. Released 24-species Pagel-lambda PGLS sensitivity fit, using log10 traits followed by sample standardization. The line and 95% model confidence ribbon are back-transformed onto measurement scales; bars are conditional measurement-bootstrap intervals. The annotation reports the standardized slope and 95% interval with an unadjusted P-value. The lambda estimate lies at the lower fitting boundary. Associations do not establish causal direction. Estimated genome size is derived from IOD and anchored to the D. fuscus assembly, not an independent species C-value; its intervals exclude anchor uncertainty. Circles show balanced multiple-image support, triangles show three one-image/one-animal estimates, and crosses flag four species failing the quality-balance gate.

**Alt text:** Genome size and nucleus area for 24 species, with measurement intervals, a fitted curve and confidence ribbon, and the released standardized slope and P-value.
